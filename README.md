# Optimising Municipal Service Delivery In Gauteng 

Over half of Gauteng households (51.5%) are dissatisfied with their local municipality, and the gap is worst in informal settlements, where sanitation dissatisfaction is about 5.6x higher than in formal housing. This project uses the GCRO Quality of Life survey (~27,000 weighted respondents) to show where services are failing and to give the provincial government a way to track whether fixes are working.

# What’s in the repo

A normalised Microsoft Access database built from the raw survey data, which had ~300 coded columns headed by question numbers. I mapped the codes to lookup tables and loaded the relevant fields.
A single-screen Power BI dashboard tracking 8 KPIs against five-year targets, with a municipality filter. The KPIs cover street lighting, roads, stormwater, billing, enquiry response rate, overall municipal satisfaction, and water and sanitation gaps between formal and informal dwellings.

 A VMOSA framework  (Vision, Mission, Objectives, Strategy, Action Plan) linking each KPI to three programmes: infrastructure, accountability and equity.

Limitations: The data is from the 2013/14 survey (QoL III), so it is a static baseline and the targets are provisional.
